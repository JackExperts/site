Jack Experts icon assets

The interface and service illustrations in this directory and ../icones are
custom artwork created for this site.

The Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube marks in ui/ preserve
the glyph outlines from the site's bundled Font Awesome 4.7 font. They are
included only to identify the corresponding services.
